Dipolar Quantum Gases

Laser intensity stabilisation for an erbium Zeeman slower.

Project Abstract

I've recently joined Dr. Rob Smith's lab to work on the laser intensity stabilisation for the Zeeman slower. The aim is to make it more stable so that our estimates for the erbium population in a quantum gas are less uncertain. We are achieving this through a PID mechanism and a motorised rotating waveplate. More to come soon!
